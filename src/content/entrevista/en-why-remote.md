Be honest, but lead with **growth and fit**, and show you understand **remote work**:

"I want to work on products used at a larger scale and learn from engineers with different backgrounds — that's hard to get in my local market right now. I've also found that I work well in a remote, **asynchronous** setup: in my projects I write design notes in the README, open detailed pull requests and document decisions, because I know that in distributed teams, clear writing replaces a lot of meetings.

In terms of logistics, I'm in Brazil (UTC−3), which gives a good overlap with US East Coast hours and most of the European afternoon, and I'm comfortable adjusting part of my schedule for team rituals."

Salary is a legitimate motivation — just don't make it the only one you mention.
