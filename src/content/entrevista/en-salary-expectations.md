"Based on my research for **remote backend roles at this level, hired as contractors from Brazil**, I'm looking at a range of **X to Y US dollars per year**. It depends on the full package, like paid time off and equipment. Is this role a contractor position or through an employer of record? And **could you share the budgeted range**?"

Some points to prepare before the call:

- **Research in dollars, not in reais.** The reference is what companies pay for remote talent in your region, not your current salary converted.
- **Know your floor.** As a contractor, you pay taxes, an accountant, health insurance and unpaid vacations. Module 17 shows how to compare offers.
- **Period and currency.** "Five thousand" can mean per month or per year, in dollars or in reais. Always say both the currency and the period.
