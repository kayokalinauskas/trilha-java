"In a code review of my project, a senior developer I asked for help told me that **my pull requests were too big to review properly**. One of them had more than 1,500 lines and mixed a refactoring with a new feature.

Honestly, **my first reaction was to defend it**: everything in the PR was related. But I asked them to show me what they meant, and they pointed out a bug they had missed on the first pass because the diff was so long. That convinced me.

**Since then**, I split work into smaller PRs, usually under 400 lines, and I keep refactoring separate from behavior changes. I also write a short description with what changed and how to test it. The next reviews came back faster and with more useful comments. That told me the change was working."
