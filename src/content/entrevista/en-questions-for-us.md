"Yes, a few. Thank you.

**What does a typical week look like for someone on this team?** For example, how much time goes into new features versus maintenance and on-call?

**What would success look like for this role in the first three to six months?**

**How does the team handle code reviews and deployments?** How long does it usually take for a change to reach production?

And one last one: **what do you enjoy most about working here**, and what would you change if you could?"

Ask an engineer about the codebase, testing and on-call. Ask a manager about expectations, feedback and growth. Ask a recruiter about the next steps and the timeline. Take notes: the answers help you compare offers later.
