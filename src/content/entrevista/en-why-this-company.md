"Three things stood out to me.

**First, the domain.** You build payment infrastructure for small businesses, and my portfolio project is a digital wallet. I've already spent time on problems like idempotent payments and correct balances under concurrency, and I'd like to work on them at real scale.

**Second, how the team works.** I read your engineering blog post about moving from a monolith to modules. I liked that you measured the impact before splitting services. That's the kind of decision-making I want to learn from.

**Third, the role itself.** The description mentions Java, Spring Boot and Kafka, and ownership of services from design to production. That's exactly where I want to grow."
